Company ne tumhein ek main IP network diya hai: 192.168.1.0/24 (Jisme total 256 IPs hain). is ip adres mai kese pata chala ki 256 ips hai ?

Yeh pata chalana boht aasan hai!

Aapko bas Slash Value (/24) ko dekhna hota hai aur Binary Math ka ek chota sa rule apply karna hota hai.



Step-by-Step Hisab (Formula)Step 1: IPv4 me Total Bits kitne hote hain?Internet Protocol version 4 (IPv4) me total 32 bits hote hain.Total Bits = 32


Step 2: Host Bits (Bachat Bits) Nikaalo
IP ke aage jo /24 likha hai, uska matlab hai ke 32 me se 24 bits Network ke naam ke liye book (lock) ho chuki hain.

Baaki kitni bits bachi jo hum apne PCs aur devices (Hosts) ko de sakte hain?

Host Bits = 32 - 24 = 8 bits

Step 3: Total IPs ka Formula LagaoNetworking me IPs humesha 2 ki power me calculate hoti hain 2^x, kyunki computer Binary (0 aur 1) samajhta hai.

Total IPs = 2^(Host Bits)

So 

Total ips =2^8=256
